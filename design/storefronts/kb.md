# K&B: front (1995 map, store s25)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).**
- **The front:** the K&B at Prien Lake Mall, Lake Charles, 1980s (refs/kb-prien-lake-mall.png). Steven: "that's exactly, exactly what the storefront at the Southland Mall looked", same owners.
- **The logo:** refs/kb-logo.jpg (straight on), refs/kb-logo-2.png and kb-logo-3.png (the round purple disc with the gold rim).
- **Superseded:** decision 6 (the pink wall with plum stripes, the entrance in the middle) and the record's tan board-and-batten.

**Accuracy level:** 3 for the front (a sister store's photo Steven vouches for), 4 for the logo. The inside is the generic one (interiors on hold).

**Front (20 m, facing −x at x = 2, z −38 to −18).**
- A brown fascia 3.0–4.6 m with the round K&B logo at each end (1.3 m: purple face, gold rim, a thin red line, white K&B traced from the logo photo), and DRUGS and TOBACCO in lit lavender-white letters with bright white edges (Francois One, OFL, 0.6 m capitals), evenly spaced.
- Viewer's left to right: display windows on a brick bulkhead (48 % of the front), a pair of aluminium-framed glass doors standing open under a transom, then glass to the floor.

**Unknown:** the third word on the Prien Lake fascia, hidden by garland (4–5 letters), is left out until Steven knows it. The font of DRUGS / TOBACCO is read off a blurry photo.

**Files:** `tools/stores/signs/fronts.gd` (kb), `make_logos.py` (kb()), materials `sg_kb_*` in `channel.gd`; hook at the top of `storefront()` in `tools/build_mall.gd`.
