## Front
- A tall all-glass front in white frames, with glass doors standing open in the middle. Window mannequins stand on a low riser.
- The sign: heavy italic yellow numerals "5·7·9", with round dots, standing off a polished steel triangle (point down) hung over the doors (video 0:33). The outline is Inter Black Italic, from `make_signs.py`.

## Inside
- **Room:** light wood plank floor (video 8:11), cream walls, lay-in ceiling with troffers.
- **Walls:** both walls are white slatwall with juniors' outfits faced out high and low (plaid skirts, denim vests, flannels, black), with folded stacks on a top shelf.
- **Middle:** round racks, white tables of folded denim, four-ways, and "NEW ARRIVALS" cards.
- **Back:** a white cash wrap on the right, and three fitting rooms with a mirror.
