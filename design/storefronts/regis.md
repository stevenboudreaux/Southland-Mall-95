# Regis Hairstylists: front and store (1995 map, store s60)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (from Steven's photos): "Regis (from the photos): honed travertine with a black cornice, triple black stripes banding the piers, a black plinth, the name in heavy black letters with light spilling round them; a tall window on the salon floor of black chairs and mirrors, the open reception with its maple desk and portraits, and walls of bottles in every colour."

**Accuracy level:** 2 to 3 for the front (the record). The inside is a period type, built on the shared kits.

**Guessed:** the depth (16 m of the 24 m stall), the station count, the door position.

**Frame.** An 8 m front facing north onto the hall at z = 60 (x −58 to −50); the salon runs 16 m south. Map tiles x 119–122, y 130–137.

## Front
- Honed travertine with a black cornice, and REGIS in heavy black letters (FreeSans Bold) with light spilling round them.
- Piers banded with three black stripes, a black plinth, and a tall window. The door is at the right.

## Inside (walkable)
- A light vinyl floor.
- A maple reception desk by the door.
- Walls of bottles, and four black styling chairs at black counters with mirrors.
