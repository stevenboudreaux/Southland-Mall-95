# Saadi's: front and store (1995 map, store s20)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 1, from Steven's photo of the sign): "Saadi's haberdashery: A black fascia; Saadi's in heavy light-grey serif, and under it to the right a grey box with haberdashery in black bold serif lowercase, a fine grey rule running in from the left to the box's middle and a small tick off its right side."

**Accuracy level:** 1 for the sign (stand-in type: DejaVu Serif Bold). The front below the sign and the inside are guesses on the flat front.

**Guessed:** the glass front, the depth (14 m of the 28 m stall), the layout and stock.

**Frame.** An 8 m front facing west at x = 2 (z 66 to 74), on the east hall, built 14 m deep. Map tiles x 149–155, y 133–136.

## Front
- Black fascia; the name in light-grey serif letters; the sub-sign (rule, grey box with "haberdashery", tick) under the right of the name.
- Glass with bronze frames, the doorway in the middle; suited forms in the windows.

## Inside (walkable)
- Wood floor, cream walls.
- Left wall: wood cubbies of shirts, then suits on warm slatwall. Right wall: suits, then a walnut tie cabinet.
- Tables of folded shirts and a round rack down the middle; the cash wrap and two fitting rooms at the back.
