# Mitchell's Formal Wear: front and store (1995 map, store s31)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 1): "the logo from the ad, black on a white panel: Mitchell's in a heavy calligraphic italic, a rule beneath it broken in the middle by a bow tie, and FORMAL WEAR in widely spaced roman capitals with the registered mark." The game's flat front (white panel on a cream front, glass, a wall of shirts in cubbies).

**Accuracy level:** 1 for the sign (stand-in type: Lora Bold Italic). The front and inside are guesses on the flat front.

**Guessed:** everything below the sign: the cream front (open question: cream or dark?), the glass front with the door on the left, the layout.

**Frame.** A 4 m front facing north at z = -6 (x 20 to 24), between Claire's and Laser Copies, built 12 m deep. Map tiles x 158–159, y 91–96.

## Front
- A cream front; the white logo panel with a dark border.
- Glass with dark frames, the door on the left; a form in a black tuxedo on a riser in the window.

## Inside (walkable)
- Grey carpet, cream walls, lay-in ceiling.
- The far wall: dinner jackets on hangers (black, white, grey), vest-and-cummerbund sets in colours, a rail of bow ties, black shoes along the bottom.
- The near wall: white cubbies of white and ivory dress shirts, a three-way mirror.
- Two forms in tuxedos on a low riser; the counter and two fitting rooms at the back.
