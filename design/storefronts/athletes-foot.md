# The Athlete's Foot: front (1995 map, store s3)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).** Steven's photo of a period Athlete's Foot front (refs/athletes-foot-front.jpg). Sign pass only: the store is still a shoe store on hold, so the generic interior stays behind the glass.

**Accuracy level:** 2 for the front (the chain's period front, not a Southland photo). The inside is the generic one.

**Front (6 m, facing −x at x = 2, z 74–80).**
- Display windows either side of an open entrance 1.9 m wide, in white frames on a grey base, with dark glass above them and a pale header.
- White square piers either side of the entrance.
- Over the entrance, a black sign box 3.1 × 1.05 m, 0.34 m deep, with a yellow pinstripe border; "The Athlete's Foot" in raised yellow letters (Fredoka Bold, OFL, each word fitted to the photo); the red foot and white wing traced from the photo.

**Guessed:** the window and pier sizes, the sign box's depth, the letters' depth.
