# Optical Outlet: front and store (1995 map, store s36)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 1, "from the Southland photo"): "a ribbed lilac-grey fascia with fat round lower-case letters glowing warm yellow; dark-framed glass and doors on a bright cream shop: walls of frames, lit cases, portraits of faces in glasses."

**Accuracy level:** 1 to 2. The name is stand-in type (Nunito Black), in title case as on the flat front.

**Guessed:** the depth (10 m), the layout, the fitting desks, the portraits (invented people).

**Frame.** An 8 m front facing south at z = 6 (x 22 to 30), beside Tee Tai's, built 10 m deep. Map tiles x 159–162, y 103–107.

## Front
- Ribbed lilac-grey fascia; the name in fat round letters glowing warm yellow.
- Dark-framed glass across the front with the doorway in the middle.

## Inside (walkable)
- Pale grey floor, cream walls, a bright lay-in ceiling.
- Walls of frames down both sides over white cabinets, a lit cornice.
- A lit case in the middle; two fitting desks with trays of frames and chairs.
- Four portraits of people in glasses on the back wall; the exam room door.
