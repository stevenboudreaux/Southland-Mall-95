# Lane Bryant: front and store (1995 map, store n10)

**Source (Oct 6, 2026).** The 1993 Hammond Square commercial (YouTube `WvaGN9lUpr0`, 5.1–6.0 s). It shows "Lane Bryant" in white lit serif letters on a cream fascia, a wide open front between cream square columns, mannequins just inside, and racks behind.

**Accuracy level:** 2. The front comes from a same-chain store of 1993. The inside is guessed.

**Guessed:** the depth (24 m of the 26 m stall), the teal carpet, the cash wrap on the right, the fitting rooms, the fixture order.

**Frame.** An 8 m front on the east hall (x = −10, z −72 to −64), beside Sound Shop. Map tiles x 131–142, y 64–67. u runs from z = −72 (the viewer's right).

## Front
- A cream panel fascia to the lane ceiling. "Lane Bryant" is in white serif letters (DejaVu Serif) with a soft halo behind them.
- Four cream square columns.
- Two white platforms just inside, each with two mannequins.
- A white soffit with downlights.

## Inside (walkable)
- Teal carpet, cream walls, a lay-in ceiling.
- White slatwall of face-outs on both walls, and the cash wrap on the right.
- One column of racks, four-ways and tables down the middle, with NEW ARRIVALS and SALE cards.
- Fitting rooms at the back.
