# Coach House Gifts: front and store (1995 map, store s21)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).**
- The Southland facade record (level 2, from Steven's photo): "a course of small black tile, a smooth grey fascia with white serif capitals, black tile piers; a deep shop with sea-green slat walls, hanging banners and a flag, tables and towers of gifts, dolls in a glass case."
- The 1993 Hammond Square commercial (15.75–16.45 s): "COACH HOUSE" in white serif capitals over one big window onto a crowded gift shop.

**Accuracy level:** 2.

**Guessed:** the depth (24 m of the 42 m stall), the door at the left end, the fixture order, the cash wrap at the back.

**Frame.** A 10 m front facing west onto the east hall (x = 2, z 16–26). Map tiles x 149–160, y 108–112.

## Front
- A smooth grey fascia with "COACH HOUSE" in white serif capitals (DejaVu Serif), over a course of small black tile.
- Black tile piers, and one big window (6.4 m) on a black tile bulkhead, with a window display of gifts.
- A 2.6 m open doorway at the left end.

## Inside (walkable)
- Grey carpet, and sea-green slatwall on the walls with shelves of gifts and cards.
- Six towers of gifts and candles down the middle.
- A glass doll case on a cherry base.
- The cash wrap at the back.
- Hanging cards: GIFTS, CARDS, COLLECTIBLES, SALE.
