# County Seat: front and store (1995 map, store n46)

**Source (Oct 6, 2026), chosen by Steven:**
- **The video:** the North East Mall video (`b1Ilr8WpkgY`). It shows the sign at 0:35 and the store at 10:50–11:12.
- **Steven:** "the inside of County Seat and that outside facade … those are just fantastic."

**Accuracy level:** 2. The sign and stock come from a same-chain store of 1995; the layout is guessed.

**Guessed:** the depth (40 m, from the map), the cash wrap in the middle, the fitting rooms across the back, and the order of the racks.

## Front
- A charcoal fascia across the 8 m unit with charcoal piers at the ends, wide open between them.
- "COUNTY·SEAT" in white raised capitals with dark returns and a centred dot (video 0:35). DejaVu Serif Bold stands in for the wide wedge-serif face.
- A dark soffit inside the opening with a row of downlights (video 10:54).

## Inside
- **Room:** white 12-inch tile with grey squares set in a pattern (video 10:54); a white lay-in ceiling with rows of 2 × 4 troffers running deep.
- **Both walls:** black slatwall with denim faced out (jackets, shirts, jeans), white cubbies of folded jeans, then more slatwall.
- **Posters:** original "DENIM" and "THE FIT" posters over the cubbies, standing in for the brand's own posters in the video.
- **Middle:** two lines of chrome four-ways and round racks, tables of folded denim, and purple "SPECIAL PRICE 25% OFF" cards hung over them (video 11:12).
- **The cash wrap:** in the middle of the store, facing the door.
- **Back:** four fitting rooms, a mirror, and the stockroom door.
