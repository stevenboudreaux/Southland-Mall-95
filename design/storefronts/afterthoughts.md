# Afterthoughts: front and store (1995 map, store n56)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (from Steven's photos): "Afterthoughts: mauve mosaic tile, raised pink speed-lines, tall white deco letters with the big round O, and a wide open front onto warm slatwall hung with bags and a spinner of earring cards || Afterthoughts lettering (from the photo): thin white lower-case, round and airy"

**Accuracy level:** 2 to 3 for the front (the record). The inside is a period type, built on the shared kits.

**Guessed:** the depth (12 m), the fixtures. The record's big round deco O is approximated by the face.

**Frame.** A 4 m front facing north onto the hall at z = 60 (x −40 to −36), beside Karmelkorn; the shop runs 12 m south. Map tiles x 128–129, y 130–135.

## Front
- Mauve mosaic tile with three raised pink speed-lines.
- "afterthoughts" in thin white lower-case letters, round and airy (Inter Light).
- A wide open front.

## Inside (walkable)
- Mauve carpet and rose walls.
- Warm slatwall of bags and accessories down both sides, a spinner of earring cards, and the counter at the back.
