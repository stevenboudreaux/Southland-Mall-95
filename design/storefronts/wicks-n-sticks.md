# Wicks 'N' Sticks: front and store (1995 map, store s44)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).**
- The Southland facade record (level 3, from photos): "a cedar-board front with a round-shouldered fascia, white rounded letters with an arch and a red flame over each word, a bay window crammed with baskets, baubles and candles on stepped oak tiers."
- The chain's history slideshow (YouTube `G8XhXFNcnAA`, 0:32–0:59): the wood front and the light-oak interior.
- Steven (Oct 6): the wood front. Notes in the project doc `claude/wicks-n-sticks-notes.md`.

**Accuracy level:** 3 for the front. The inside follows the chain's photos.

**Guessed:** the depth (12 m, the stall), the fixture count, the counter at the back.

**Frame.** A 6 m front facing south onto the hall at z = 48 (x −38 to −32); the store runs north. Map tiles x 129–131, y 118–123.

## Front
- Dark-stained vertical-groove cedar boards to the lane ceiling.
- A black panel fascia with round shoulders, "Wicks 'N' Sticks" in white rounded letters (Nunito Black), a white arch over each word, and a red-orange flame in each arch.
- A bay window on a cedar sill, crammed with candles on three stepped oak tiers.
- An open door bay on the right.

## Inside (walkable)
- Mauve carpet and wood-panelled walls.
- Light-oak wall shelving of candles and gifts.
- Two stepped oak tables piled with candles.
- A counter at the back, and warm amber light.
