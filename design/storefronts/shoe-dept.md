# The Shoe Dept: front (1995 map, store s51)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).** Steven's five photos (refs/shoe-dept-1..5): "that's like the exact facade". Sign pass only; the generic interior stays (shoe stores on hold).

**Accuracy level:** 3 for the front (Steven vouches for it), the photos being later than 1995.

**Front (16 m, facing +z at z = 44).** Dark brown-grey marble on the fascia and the end piers; "the SHOE DEPT." in white lit letters (SHOE DEPT traced from refs/shoe-dept-2.jpg, 0.62 m capitals; "the" in Kaushan Script, OFL; a square full stop); a brass edge under the fascia; brass round columns either side of a 3.7 m open entrance; display windows either side in brass frames on brass bases.

**Guessed:** the widths, the column size, the marble pattern.
