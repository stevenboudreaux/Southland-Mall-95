# Gordon's Jewelers: front and store (1995 map, store s29)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (from Steven's photos): "Gordon's Jewelers, from photographs of the shop: a dark bronze header holding a row of back-lit frosted panels crossed by one long arch, the name on the centre panel; under it an open front onto a dim taupe room hung with framed pictures under track lights, and lit glass cases on a dark mahogany base. The name is drawn by hand as outlines in the sign's narrow, high-contrast serif: thick sides and hairline tops to the round letters, flat serifs."

**Accuracy level:** 2 to 3 for the front (the record). The inside is a period type, built on the shared kits.

**Guessed:** the layout and the case count.

**Frame.** A corner shop facing west onto the east hall (x = 2, z −18 to −10) and south onto the cross hall at z = −10 (x 2 to 12). Built as 8 m × 10 m. Map tiles x 149–153, y 91–94.

## Front
- A dark bronze header on both fronts, holding a row of back-lit frosted panels crossed by one long arch, with Gordon's and JEWELERS in a high-contrast serif on the centre panel (painted, lit).
- Bronze piers, and open fronts.

## Inside (walkable)
- A dim taupe room on taupe carpet, under warm light.
- Lit glass cases on dark wood bases, with trays of rings, chains and watches.
- Framed pictures.
