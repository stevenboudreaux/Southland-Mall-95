# General Nutrition Center (GNC): front and store (1995 map, store s21c)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 2, from Steven's photo): "a portal of honed stone tile with the three red letters standing off the lintel, glass either side on black plinths stacked with tubs, a banner in the window, and the bright shop of shelves beyond." Hammond 1993 (27.3 s) and Valley View 1995 (0:37) show GNC's red letters in the period.

**Accuracy level:** 2 for the front. The inside is guessed.

**Guessed:** the depth (14 m of the 30 m stall), white wall bays of bottles and tubs, the counter across the back, the cards.

**Frame.** A 4 m front facing west onto the east hall (x = 2, z 34–38). Map tiles x 149–155, y 117–118. u runs from z = 34 (the viewer's left).

## Front
- Beige honed stone tile above the opening, with "GNC" in red lit letters standing off it (FreeSans Bold).
- A stone-jambed doorway, 1.4 m wide.
- Glass either side on black plinths, with tubs and bottles stacked behind.

## Inside (walkable)
- A light vinyl floor, white walls, a lay-in ceiling.
- White wall bays of bottles, tubs and boxes down both sides.
- A counter with a register across the back.
- GOLD CARD and SALE cards hung from the ceiling.
