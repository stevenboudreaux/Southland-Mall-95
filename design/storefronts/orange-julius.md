# Orange Julius: front and store (1995 map, store s34)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (from Steven's photos): "Orange Julius (from the photo): cream tile, a raised sign board with the name in looping orange neon, a course of red tiles set with black diamonds; inside, pale walls hung with a row of framed prints, the tiled counter under black menu light-boxes with the steel of the kitchen behind, oak booths either side, a dark red floor, a queue post, a brick pier."

**Accuracy level:** 2 to 3 for the front (the record). The inside is a period type, built on the shared kits.

**Guessed:** the depth (6 m: Coach House's room is behind it), the layout. The oak booths, the queue post and the brick pier are not built yet.

**Frame.** A 6 m front facing north onto the hall at z = 10 (x 10 to 16), backing onto Coach House Gifts; built 6 m deep. Map tiles x 153–155, y 105–107.

## Front
- Cream tile, and a raised cream sign board with "Orange Julius" in looping orange neon (traced from Mr Dafoe).
- A course of red tiles set with black diamonds.

## Inside (walkable)
- A dark red tile floor and pale walls.
- The tiled counter with its diamond course and a register.
- Black menu light-boxes, and the kitchen's steel behind.
- A framed print.
