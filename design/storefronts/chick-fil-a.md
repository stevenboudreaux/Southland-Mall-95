# Chick-fil-A: front and restaurant (1995 map, store s64)

**Sources (Oct 7, 2026).**
- The Southland facade record (level 2, from Steven's photo): "a bulkhead of tan tile set on the diagonal, a deep red sign box with the script name in hot pink-white neon, … below, the long timber counter with dark inset rounds under a row of [menu panels]."
- The 1993 Hammond Square commercial (18.9–19.6 s): a mall Chick-fil-A dining room with striped cream wallpaper, brass sconces, framed pictures and tables.

**Accuracy level:** 2.

**Guessed:** the depth (16 m of the 40 m stall), the open front, the dining room in front of the counter, the menu panels' content (invented lines and prices).

**Left out:** the chain's mascot details in the record's sign (the C's comb and beak). The script name is traced in neon from an open script face (Mr Dafoe).

**Frame.** An 8 m front facing north onto the hall at z = 60 (x −102 to −94), between Pocket Change and County Seat; the restaurant runs south. Map tiles x 97–100, y 130–137.

## Front
- Tan tile set on the diagonal over and beside the opening.
- A deep red sign box with "Chick-fil-A" in hot pink-white neon script.
- An open front under a tan soffit with downlights.

## Inside (walkable)
- A warm floor. Striped cream wallpaper over a dark wood wainscot, brass sconces with globe shades, framed food pictures.
- Six white pedestal tables with red chairs.
- The long timber counter across the back, with dark inset rounds and two registers. Four lit menu panels on the back wall, and a stainless kitchen line behind.
