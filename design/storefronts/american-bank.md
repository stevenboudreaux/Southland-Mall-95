# American Bank: front and lobby (1995 map, store s29ab)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 1, from a grainy black-and-white photograph of the Southland branch): "a white stucco front, the name in heavy rounded white letters on the shaded wall above, one wide arch closed by a wrought-iron gate, and a black carriage lantern on each side. The photograph is black and white, so the colours are a guess."

**Accuracy level:** 1. Stand-in type (Nunito Black).

**Guessed:** the colours; the lobby behind the gate (teller line with glass, a desk, stanchions); whether the gate was closed in the daytime (open question; it is closed in both lightings).

**Frame.** A 10 m front facing north onto the Sears hall at z = -86 (x -46 to -36), built 8 m deep. The gate is closed: nothing walkable.

## Front
- White stucco with one wide arch; a shaded band above it with the name in heavy rounded white letters, a small cornice.
- A wrought-iron gate in the arch; a black carriage lantern each side.

## Behind the gate
- A pale lobby floor, the teller line in walnut with glass, a desk, brass stanchions with a red rope.
