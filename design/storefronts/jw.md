# JW / Jeans West: front and store (1995 map, store n33)

**Source (Oct 6, 2026), chosen by Steven:**
- **The video:** "1995 Mall commercial (North East Mall 80s, 90s mall)", YouTube `b1Ilr8WpkgY`. It shows the JW sign at 0:25, the front at 2:50 (beside Gumballs) and the store at 3:44–4:02.
- **Steven:** the inside is "fantastic … the layout and where all of the racks were and the cash register … that's the key".

**Accuracy level:** 2. The sign and stock come from a same-chain store of 1995; the layout is guessed.

**Guessed:** the depth (28 m, from the map), the cash wrap on the right wall, the fitting rooms across the back, the fixture order, and the front's glass and doorway.

## Front
- A charcoal glass front with a dark header band. On it, "JW" in a tall serif traced in double white neon, with a neon bar over it (video 0:25). The outline is DejaVu Serif Bold, from `make_signs.py`.
- Two show windows with mannequins (a grey suit; khakis) and an open doorway between them.
- A dark soffit with three downlights.

## Inside
- **Room:** white tile floor, cream walls, lay-in ceiling with 2 × 4 troffers.
- **Left wall:** black slatwall with men's and women's casual wear faced out in two tiers, folded stacks on a top shelf, then white cubbies of folded jeans.
- **Right wall:** slatwall, then the cash wrap (a black counter with a register), then more slatwall.
- **Middle:** a platform of two mannequins by the door, chrome round racks, tables of folded jeans and khakis, and four-ways.
- **Signs:** the blue "TOPS 2 for $20 PANTS" banners hanging over the racks (video 3:46), and a "JEANS" header.
- **Back:** three fitting rooms with louvred doors, a mirror, and the stockroom door.
