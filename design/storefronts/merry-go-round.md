# Merry-Go-Round: front and store (1995 map, store s47)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (from Steven's photos): "Merry-Go-Round (from the photos): a gloss-black fascia with fat round tube letters, MERRY red, GO yellow, ROUND green; dark-framed windows either side, and a bright open front: sunset stripes on the wall, the I.O.U. banner hung across, rails of jackets and tops, a round rack of striped shirts out front, a red-skirted table, sale boards on easels, glossy wood."

**Accuracy level:** 2 to 3 for the front (the record). The inside is a period type, built on the shared kits.

**Guessed:** the depth (20 m of the 28 m stall), the cash wrap and fitting rooms, the fixture order. The I.O.U. banner (a separate brand) is left out.

**Frame.** An 8 m front facing south onto the hall at z = 48 (x −102 to −94), beside B. Dalton; the store runs 20 m north. Map tiles x 97–100, y 114–123.

## Front
- A gloss-black fascia with MERRY in red, GO in yellow and ROUND in green: fat rounded tube letters, lit (Nunito Black outlines). The hyphens are left out.
- Dark-framed windows either side, with mannequins, and an open front under a black soffit.

## Inside (walkable)
- A wood floor and cream walls, with sunset stripes high on the back wall.
- Black slatwall of face-outs on both sides, and the cash wrap on the right.
- Round racks, four-ways and tables down the middle, with sale cards, and two fitting rooms.
