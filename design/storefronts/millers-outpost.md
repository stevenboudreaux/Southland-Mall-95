# Miller's Outpost: front and store (1995 map, store s17)

**Source (Oct 6, 2026).** The 1992 Pecanland Mall opening-day video (YouTube `8ZnqaDZMufk`, 9:13–9:19). It shows "Miller's Outpost" in orange-red serif letters, outlined, with a star between the words, on a wood fascia over a wide open front between wood piers, with racks and wall stock of jeans and tops inside. The facade record (level 3) agrees.

**Accuracy level:** 2. A same-chain store of 1992. The inside is guessed.

**Guessed:** the depth (28 m of the 42 m stall), the cash wrap on the right, the fitting rooms, the fixture order.

**Frame.** A 12 m front on the east hall (x = −10, z −52 to −40), beside Kay-Bee Toys. Map tiles x 129–142, y 74–79. u runs from z = −52 (the viewer's right, Kay-Bee's side).

## Front
- A warm wood fascia to the lane ceiling.
- "Miller's ★ Outpost" in raised orange-red serif letters (DejaVu Serif Bold) with cream returns, and a cream star.
- Open between three wood piers, under a wood soffit with downlights.

## Inside (walkable)
- A wood plank floor, cream walls.
- Black slatwall of face-outs on both walls, and the cash wrap on the right.
- Two columns of round racks, four-ways and tables of folded jeans.
- JEANS $19.99 and 25% OFF cards.
- Fitting rooms at the back.
