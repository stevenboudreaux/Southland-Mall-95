# Golden Chain Gang: front and counter (1995 map, store s62)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 1): "the shop's own mark, traced from its 1994 newspaper advertisement (three big outlined G's down the left, GOLDEN / CHAIN / GANG beside them, a chain running down to a ball), painted in gold on a black front; below it an open counter of lit cases and chains on display busts. Colours are a guess: the ad is black and white."

**Accuracy level:** 1. The mark is redrawn in stand-in type (Fraunces initials, DejaVu Serif words); it follows the flat front's G, C, G initials (open question: the record says three G's).

**Guessed:** the colours, the depth (8 m of the 40 m stall), the velvet room behind the counter.

**Frame.** A 6 m front facing south at z = 60 (x -74 to -68), by the Concourse, built 8 m deep. A counter shop: shoppers stand in the hall, so no tiles are opened.

## Front
- A black front with the gold mark across it, a gold line at the head.
- A counter of lit glass cases right at the front line, three velvet busts with gold chains on top.

## Behind the counter
- Dark velvet walls hung with gold chains in V's on lit panels; a back counter with three more busts.
