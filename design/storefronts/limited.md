# The Limited: front and store (1995 map, store s50)

**Source (Oct 6, 2026).** The 1993 Hammond Square commercial (YouTube `WvaGN9lUpr0`, 3.7–4.1 s). It shows a black capsule sign with chrome ends and "the limited" in white neon script, over an open front packed with racks of pink and white clothes and a "1/2" sign.

**Accuracy level:** 2. A same-chain store of 1993. The inside is guessed.

**Guessed:** the room behind the 10 m main front only, 28 m deep. The three 2 m stepped edges to its west are plain mall wall. Also guessed: the grey carpet, the white walls, the cash wrap on the right, the fitting rooms, the fixture order.

**Frame.** The main front faces the hall to the south at z = 48 (x −132 to −122); the store runs north (−z). Map tiles x 82–86, y 110–123.

## Front
- A charcoal fascia. A black capsule, 5.6 m × 0.8 m, with rounded chrome ends, and "the limited" traced in white neon (Inter Bold Italic outlines).
- An open front between two charcoal piers, under a charcoal soffit with downlights.

## Inside (walkable)
- Grey carpet, white walls, a lay-in ceiling.
- White slatwall of face-outs on both walls, and the cash wrap on the right.
- Two columns of racks, four-ways and tables, with 1/2 PRICE, NEW, $19 and SALE cards.
- Fitting rooms at the back.
