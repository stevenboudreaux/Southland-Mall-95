# Tee Tai's: front and counter (1995 map, store s35)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (level 1): "a black sign box with the name in pale gold brush script (long sweeping bars on the two T's), a black fretwork rail under it, and red paper lanterns with gold bands and tassels hung along the front." The game's flat front (menu boards with food pictures, a red counter with gold medallions).

**Accuracy level:** 1 for the front (stand-in type: Mr Dafoe, which lacks the long sweeping bars on the T's). The counter is a period type.

**Guessed:** the depth (8 m), the layout, the menu: the dish names (Bourbon Chicken, Sweet & Sour Pork, Pepper Steak, Lo Mein, Fried Rice, Broccoli Beef) and the prices (egg roll .99, two-item plate 3.99, three-item plate 4.69) are guesses for a 1995 Louisiana mall counter.

**Frame.** A 6 m front facing south at z = 6 (x 16 to 22), beside Optical Outlet, built 8 m deep. The customers' strip in front of the counter is walkable: map tiles x 156–158, y 103.

## Front
- A black sign box with the name in pale gold script; a black fretwork rail (key pattern) under it.
- Three round red paper lanterns with gold bands and tassels along the front.

## Inside
- A red tile floor, cream walls.
- The lacquer red counter with gold medallions, a steam table of food under a sneeze guard, a register.
- Three back-lit menu boards hung over the back of the counter; the kitchen line (steel, woks, a hood) behind.
