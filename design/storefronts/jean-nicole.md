# Jean Nicole: front and store (1995 map, store s16)

**Source (Oct 7, 2026).** The Southland facade record (from Steven's photos): "Jean Nicole (from the photos): a rust-red fascia with the name in fat white brush script, a yellow dot over the i; a mirrored soffit, a red shop on red carpet: round racks of pastel blouses, maroon sale cards in chrome frames on stands, forms in pastel suits up on the fixtures."

**Accuracy level:** 2 to 3 for the front (the record). The inside is a period type, built on the shared kits.

**Guessed:** the depth (24 m of the 42 m stall), the cash wrap and fitting rooms, the fixture order.

**Frame.** A 10 m front on the east hall (x = −10, z −40 to −30), between Miller's Outpost and the hall's end stores; the store runs 24 m west. Map tiles x 131–142, y 80–84.

## Front
- A rust-red fascia with "Jean Nicole" in fat white brush script (Mr Dafoe, OFL) and a yellow dot over the i.
- A mirrored soffit, and rust-framed windows either side of the doorway, with mannequins in pastels.

## Inside (walkable)
- Red carpet and rose walls, with white slatwall of face-outs and the cash wrap on the left.
- Two columns of round racks, four-ways and tables, with sale cards, and fitting rooms at the back.
